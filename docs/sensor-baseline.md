Dataset `dummy-sensors-v1`, validation session `sensor-validation`, configuration `impact-rules-v1`; two boards, four nominal 800 Hz channels, 10.1 synthetic scheduled seconds. Separate train and public blind fixtures use distinct raw signals/boot/incident/group identities. All waveforms and fit/holdout markers were generated by software.

The 7/8 detection count **includes two timed unknown events**; it is not class accuracy or goal recall. The third unknown event has no host-clock mapping after reboot and cannot match the timed reference, which remains a miss. Its device time and raw signal stay available. Zero unmatched timed impacts over ten artificial seconds gives a descriptive Poisson 95% upper bound of approximately **1,315 impacts/hour**, not evidence of a low field false-impact rate. Unmapped events are reported separately. Latency uses the synthetic label-to-evidence receipt timeline, includes the causal follow-up and packetization, and never measures display or goal confirmation.

There are no learned classes or calibrated probabilities. Wall/frame share a cue; broad dummy player motion fits the low-roughness rule, not proven player recognition. Every event preserves possible-goal status and forbids automatic confirmation. A frame/player-looking sensor cue cannot veto a valid visual crossing.

Injected failures: one lost batch, one CRC-corrupted batch, garbage bytes, one FIFO-overrun flag, clipping, a virtual disconnect and reboot without a replacement alignment. The decoder reports one CRC error and 279 discarded bytes; host reports two missing packets, 64 missing transmitted samples, 13 near-rail samples, one reboot and one transport reset. Actual ADC losses implied by the overrun flag are unquantified. Post-and-in produces mixed evidence with possible goal retained. Final simulated timeout reports stale sensors; separate tests verify heartbeat-only streams cannot hide lost samples and a bad board does not stop the other reader.

## Timing and offline service time

[Timing JSON](sensor-timing-results.json): each initial boot has three fit and three later holdout markers. Generated drift is 0/100 ppm for boards 1/2, with a maximum held-out mapping bound of 1 ms per board over device seconds 0–9.998. Adding the dummy packet's nominal 1.25 ms phase uncertainty gives approximately 2.25 ms sample uncertainty. These values are constructed; no physical shared-marker/FIFO-phase experiment occurred. New boot 1101 has no mapping and stays unmapped.

[Offline throughput](sensor-throughput.json): one short validation replay, including asset hashes, CRC, host conversion, classification and metrics, processed 31,936 samples in 1.612 seconds, approximately 19,808 samples/s versus the configured combined 3,200 samples/s input rate. This uses local files and no serial devices, live producer/queue, capture/inference contention or sustained resource measurement. It does not verify firmware/USB/host deployment capacity. The raw dummy bundles total 743,784 bytes across three sessions, plus small receipt/manifest files.
