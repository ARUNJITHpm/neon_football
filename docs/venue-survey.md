## Camera challenge procedure

1. Record the actual venue lighting, camera settings and fixed mounting position.
2. Film slow rolls, hard shots, partial crossings, high balls, rebounds, post-and-in, post-and-out, outside-net impacts and player occlusions at each end.
3. Identify whether footage distinguishes the whole ball crossing within the opening from near misses; label ambiguous cases explicitly.
4. Preserve raw files with session IDs and timestamps. The operator goal button is a rough marker, not crossing ground truth.
5. Record visibility limitations and whether an additional/repositioned camera is necessary.

Acceptance evidence: measured configuration, agreed venue rules, labelled challenge examples from both ends and a costed prototype configuration. No recording or field test has been performed by this software implementation.
